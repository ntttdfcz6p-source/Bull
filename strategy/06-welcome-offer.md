# First-Purchase Welcome Offer Concept

Inspired by the strategic *role* AG1's Welcome Kit plays (make the first commitment feel like more value than the price), not a copy of its contents. Only realistically sourceable items included.

## Offer: "The BullRush Start Kit"
Available only on a **first Subscribe & Save order** (any bundle size).

| Included | Normal value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| First subscription shipment at the intro subscriber price | Reflects the discount shown in `05-pricing-framework.md` | Real, disclosed pricing |
| Free branded travel case | `$[PLACEHOLDER value]` | Physical item — must be sourced/quoted before promising |
| Bonus digital "Men's Daily Performance Guide" (PDF) | `$[PLACEHOLDER value]` | Digital — zero marginal cost, safe to include immediately once written |
| Free shipping on the first order | Actual shipping cost | Real, disclosed |

**Total stated value vs. price paid** must always be shown as: *(sum of real, sourced item values) vs. (actual first-order subscribe price)* — never an invented "you're getting $250 of value" number with no line-item backup.

## Terms shown at point of offer (not buried)
- This is a **subscription** offer: after the first shipment, it renews at the standard recurring subscribe price shown on the purchase module, at the frequency selected.
- Skip, pause, or cancel anytime before the next billing date from the account portal.
- Travel case and Performance Guide are one-time welcome items, not repeated on future shipments.
- Offer applies to new subscribers only, one per customer.

## Sequencing
1. Homepage / DR landing page mention the Start Kit briefly under the hero or near the pricing module ("New subscribers get the Start Kit — details at checkout").
2. Product offer page purchase module shows the full offer breakdown once "Subscribe & Save" tab is active.
3. Cart drawer confirms what's included.
4. Order-confirmation and welcome email restate the terms in full (see `11-klaviyo-flows.md`, Welcome/Subscription-Onboarding flow).

## What's intentionally excluded until real assets exist
No branded apparel, no physical guidebook, no multi-item "kit box" — these require sourcing/inventory decisions outside this build's scope. The framework above is designed so any of these can be added later without restructuring the offer logic.
