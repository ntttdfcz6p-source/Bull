# A/B Test Backlog

Grouped by surface. Prioritize tests that touch the primary purchase decision first.

## Purchase module (homepage, DR landing page, product offer page — same component, test once)
- Default active tab: One-Time vs. Subscribe & Save
- Recommended-tier badge copy: "Most Popular" vs. "Best Value" vs. "Recommended"
- Tier identity naming: "Build Consistency" vs. a plainer "3 Bottles" control
- Price-per-day display vs. price-per-bottle-only display
- Position of the subscription disclosure line (directly under CTA vs. inside an expandable "Subscription details")

## Hero / headline (DR landing page primarily, since it's built for testing)
- "YOUR DAILY POWER." vs. "DRIVE DOESN'T COME FROM NOWHERE." vs. "BUILT FOR THE DAYS THAT DEMAND MORE."
- Hero CTA copy: "CHOOSE YOUR SUPPLY" vs. "START YOUR ROUTINE" vs. "FUEL YOUR POWER"
- Presence/absence of a rating badge directly under the hero CTA (once real reviews exist)

## Proof placement
- Problem-proof section before vs. after product-intro
- Review/UGC section position: immediately after hero vs. immediately before pricing

## Objection handling (DR landing page)
- Order of objections answered (safety-first vs. differentiation-first vs. guarantee-first)
- Standalone FAQ accordion vs. sequential objection-answer blocks

## Welcome offer
- Leading with the discount vs. leading with the bonus items (travel case + guide)
- Value framing: itemized real value list vs. single "Start Kit" bundle name only

## Sticky mobile CTA
- Persistent bar showing recommended tier price vs. showing "From $[price/bottle]"
- Bar appears immediately vs. appears after a scroll-depth threshold

## Guardrail for every test
No test may introduce a fabricated price, review count, or claim as a variant — testing is limited to framing, layout, sequencing, and real-data presentation, never to inventing content solely to see if it converts better.
